# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Facility managers, hotel and restaurant owners, hospital administrators, school bursars and factory admins in Coimbatore, Chennai and the rest of Tamil Nadu. They are choosing or renewing a fire-safety vendor — at a desk in daylight, or on a phone during a site walk — and need to know three things fast: is this company certified (BIS, ISI, NABH, NBC compliance), does it supply / install / refill / maintain what my building needs, and how do I reach a person now.

## Product Purpose
The marketing site for Universal Fire Safety Equipments: supplier, installer and maintainer of fire extinguishers, alarm, hydrant, suppression and burglar-alarm systems, plus AMC, refilling, training and fire-licence services. Success is a call, a WhatsApp enquiry, or a survey request — and the visitor leaving convinced the company is exact about compliance.

## Brand Personality
Calm, certified, exact. The people who are already there before the spark. Voice is plain, specific and unhurried — specs and standards stated as facts, never hype. Emotional goal: reassurance through competence.

## Anti-references
- The current WordPress/Elementor site: red header bar, flip boxes, carousels, spun SEO prose.
- Emergency-siren aesthetics: red/black/yellow hazard stripes, flame clip-art, "URGENT!" energy.
- Generic corporate-safety templates: stock handshake photos, icon-card grids, stat tiles.
- Black + vermilion "dark mode agency" looks.

## Design Principles
1. **Prove, don't promise.** Standards, specs and real photographs carry the claims; adjectives don't.
2. **One fire, put out.** The only drama on the site is the extinguish moment on the home page; everything after it is foam-calm.
3. **The phone number is never more than a glance away.** Call and WhatsApp are in the nav, the footer, and every page's end.
4. **Exact over loud.** Tabular figures, real units, aligned specs — precision is the visual voice.
5. **Readable without motion.** Every animated state has a finished, static default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on its own ground. Reduced motion is a designed variant (the extinguish scene shows its finished state). Content visible with JS off. Pinch-zoom never blocked. Touch targets ≥ 44px. Phone numbers are real `tel:` links. Many visitors are on mid-range Android phones on mobile data — keep the scene light.
